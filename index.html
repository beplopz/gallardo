Topic 15

Battery Life Test

Measure laptop battery life under different usage scenarios and compare the results with desktop power consumption.

Experiment Goal

What this test compares

This project checks how long a fully charged laptop can operate during common activities, then compares those results with how much power a desktop computer uses for similar tasks.

Conclusion

Key Finding

Laptops last longer during light tasks like browsing, while heavy tasks such as gaming drain the battery quickly. Desktops do not rely on a battery, but they usually consume more electricity, especially during demanding workloads.
